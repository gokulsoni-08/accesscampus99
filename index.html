What IIMA says about accessibility

IIMA states that it aims to provide an enabling environment: ramps and railings, high-power lighting and high-contrast signage for students with low vision, and elevators that help differently-abled students use the underpass connecting the heritage and new campuses.

Source: iima.ac.in – Community Services

Data status: IIMA does not publish a location-level list of ramps, lifts, accessible toilets or parking. Everything below those official statements is marked Unverified until someone checks it on site.

Record a step-free path

Walk a route you know is step-free (ramps / lifts only) and the app saves the GPS trail on this device. Recorded paths show as green on the map.

    Walking routes come from the free public OSRM foot-routing service and do not know about steps or ramps. They are shown as amber dashed lines and are never described as wheelchair-safe. IIMA states that the underpass has elevators, so routes between the two campuses are sent via the underpass.
